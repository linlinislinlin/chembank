  A undergoes an intramolecular aldol reaction on heating with dilute base to
  form a cyclic product B.
  (b) Draw the mechanism for the formation of B from A.
answer: >-
  Base removes an alpha-H from the CH2 between the two carbonyls to give an
  enolate; the enolate attacks the ketone carbonyl intramolecularly to form a
  six-membered ring; protonation gives the beta-hydroxy ketone B.
