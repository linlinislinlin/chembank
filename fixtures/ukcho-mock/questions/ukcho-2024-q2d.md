  Silver also crystallises with an fcc unit cell, with a = 409 pm
  (Ar: Cu = 63.5, Ag = 108).
  (d) Predict, with justification, whether the density of silver is greater or
  less than that of copper.
answer: >-
  Density = (4 x Ar) / (N_A x a^3). Silver has larger Ar but a larger cell
  volume (a^3 rises with the cube of 409/361 ~ 1.45); the ratio Ar/a^3 is
  108/(1.45 x 63.5) ~ 1.17, so silver is denser. Justification must compare
  both the mass and volume changes, not Ar alone.
mark_scheme: >-
  M1 uses density proportional to Ar/a^3; M2 evaluates the comparison;
  M3 conclusion: silver is denser (greater).
teacher_notes: >-
