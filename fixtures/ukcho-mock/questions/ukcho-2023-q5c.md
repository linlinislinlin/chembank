  (c) Suggest a two-step synthesis of C from B, giving reagents and conditions,

answer: >-
  Step 1: convert the carboxylic acid to a less reactive derivative or protect it
  (e.g. esterify with ethanol/H2SO4) so the ketone is not attacked. Step 2:
  dehydrate under base (e.g. dilute NaOH, heat) to give the enone C. Selectivity:
  the acid must be protected because base would otherwise deprotonate it and
  compete with enolate formation.
