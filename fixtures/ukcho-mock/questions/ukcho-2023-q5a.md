question_text: >-
  Compound A has molecular formula C7H12O3. Its IR spectrum shows a broad
  absorption at 2500-3300 cm-1 and a strong band at 1710 cm-1. Its 1H NMR
  spectrum shows: delta 1.2 (3H, triplet), 2.4 (2H, quartet), 2.6 (2H, singlet),
  3.7 (2H, singlet), and a broad signal at 11.0 (1H, exchangeable).
  (a) Deduce the structure of A. Justify each piece of evidence.
answer: >-
  Broad 2500-3300 cm-1 + exchangeable 11.0 ppm = COOH. 1710 cm-1 = carbonyl.
  Triplet/quartet pair at 1.2/2.4 = CH3CH2 next to a carbonyl. Singlet at 3.7
  (2H) = CH2 next to COOH; singlet at 2.6 (2H) = CH2 between two carbonyls.
  A = CH3CH2COCH2CH2COOH (4-oxohexanoic acid), which can cyclise.
mark_scheme: >-
  M1 identifies COOH from IR + 11.0 ppm; M2 identifies CH3CH2CO- from the
  triplet/quartet; M3 places the two isolated CH2 groups; M4 assigns full
  structure consistent with C7H12O3.
