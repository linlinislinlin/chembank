## Question

1 Which contains the largest number of hydrogen atoms?
A 0.10mol of pentane
B 0.20mol of but-2-ene
C 1.00mol of hydrogen molecules
D 6.02× 10²³ hydrogen atoms

## Mark Scheme

Answer: **C**

| Option | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| A | 0.10 mol C5H12 → 1.2 mol H atoms |
| B | 0.20 mol C4H8 → 1.6 mol H atoms |
| C | 1.00 mol H2 → **2.0 mol H atoms** (largest) |
| D | 6.02×10²³ atoms = 1.0 mol H atoms |
