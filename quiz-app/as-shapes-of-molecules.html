Explore common molecular shapes in AS Chemistry. Compare bond pairs and lone pairs, view polished 3D-style models with stronger perspective, and build your own structure.

Key Idea

Electron pairs around the central atom repel each other and arrange themselves as far apart as possible. The strength of repulsion is: lone pair – lone pair > lone pair – bond pair > bond pair – bond pair.
